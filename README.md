# THE ARMS RACE &middot; the loser learns most

> A sphere of **UD0** — the ROOT0 universe / biosphere. Domain: **POLEMOS**.

THE ARMS RACE &mdash; ENYO's, on David's insight: you learn by being BROKEN, the strife is the teacher. Two rivals in a war neither can win; each round the LOSER learns most (its gain grows with how far behind it was &mdash; convex catch-up), so the pair stays neck-and-neck (the Red Queen: run just to stay in place) yet both climb far past what either becomes in peace. Built on the 3-corner layout: a 2D ledger (the two strengths climbing above the flat 'peace' baseline), a 3D clash (two forces rushing in and rebounding), and an EXPANDED toddler SPARRING ARENA (tap two fighters &mdash; the loser flashes 'LEARNED +X' and levels up, a live ledger prints the height the conflict bought over peace). &#9670; LIT: the loser-learns-most rule and the surplus-over-peace are exact/measured (node-verified: 200 rounds &rarr; gap stays ~1, mean +128 above peace); documented as the Red Queen (Van Valen 1973), evolutionary arms races and adversarial (GAN) training. &#9650; AMBER: the strength units and learn-constants are a toy model; real arms races can also deadlock or ruin both sides.
